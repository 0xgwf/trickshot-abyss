## Unofficial project

Trickshot Abyss (弹无虚坑) is an unofficial fan project whose gameplay is a tribute to _BALL x PIT_ (Chinese title 《球比伦战记》), developed by Kenny Sun and published by Devolver Digital. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Kenny Sun, Devolver Digital or their affiliates. "BALL x PIT", "球比伦战记" and related names are trademarks of their respective owners.

No art, audio or code from the original game is used. All graphics and sounds in this project are generated by code, and the original game was not unpacked or decompiled.
